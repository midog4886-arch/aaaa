---
name: Manual campaign inquiry boundaries
description: Why social-ad inquiry follow-up stays separate from automated registration follow-ups
---

Social-ad inquiry tracking is manual and must not enroll contacts in registration follow-up automation. Opening WhatsApp is not evidence of contact; staff explicitly log completed contact.

**Why:** The user requested organized follow-up for existing social-media inquiries, including branches without Whatsflow. Importing numbers authorizes storage for review, not automated outreach.

**How to apply:** Preserve this separation when adding import sources or messaging features. Deduplicate contacts within a branch, not across branches. Invoice conversion must use an explicitly linked invoice, never phone matching, because families can share numbers.
