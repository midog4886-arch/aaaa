---
name: Schedule edits preserve earned calendar opportunities
description: Distinguish future schedule changes, retrospective corrections, absences and prior repairs.
---

Schedule changes must preserve paid entitlement independently of the current weekday frequency. Future-only changes redistribute existing future attendable opportunities, not every unused paid session; elapsed absence is not compensation.

**Why:** changing weekdays after off-day attendance can leave old deadline deductions in place. Simply extending until all unused sessions fit also reimburses absences, contrary to the approved rule.

**How to apply:** preview the before/after deadline and require confirmation. Retrospective corrections need evidence that a historical deduction became obsolete. Preserve invoice and attendance history, and record which deductions were neutralized so deleting attendance cannot reverse them again.

Prior manual expiry repairs must be considered before any retrospective compensation. If their evidence does not identify restored deductions, block automatic correction rather than infer a second award.

**Why:** a repaired deadline already contains restored opportunities even when old attendance retains off-day flags. A paid-quota cap alone does not prevent double compensation when absences leave unused quota.

**How to apply:** bind previews to repair evidence and require separate review when provenance is ambiguous. Do not bulk-repair historical subscriptions merely because this protection is added.
